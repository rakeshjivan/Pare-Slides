# Pare Slides

Put your slides on screen at the right moments in a recorded talk, then finish it in Camtasia.

1. Drop in the **recording**: a video or audio file (MP4, MOV, M4A, MP3, WAV).
2. Drop in the **slides**: a zip or folder of slide images, or a PDF of the deck.
3. Click **Match Slides to Recording**. Pare Slides takes out the sound, transcribes the whole recording on your Mac, reads every slide, and works out when each slide should come up.
4. Check the change points. Play from any change, nudge it by half a second, type an exact time, swap the slide, add or remove a change, or click a line of the transcript to change slide there.
5. **Export Camtasia Project…** gives you a `.cmproj` with the slides on track 1, the sound on track 2, and every slide in the Media Bin. **Export MP4…** gives you a finished video.

## Matching

- With **Claude connected** (the button at the top right: sign in with Google, once per Mac), Claude reads the full transcript and the full deck and picks the change points. It uses your Claude subscription through Claude Code.
- Without Claude, Pare Slides matches on the words in the slides and what is said, on your Mac.

Transcription runs on the Mac and uses Apple's speech recogniser. The newest engine is used on macOS 26.

## Install

Download `Pare-Slides-mac.zip` from the latest release, unzip it, and drag **Pare Slides** into Applications. The first time you open it, right-click it and choose **Open**. It updates itself.
